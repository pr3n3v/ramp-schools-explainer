Example words

Every one of the 44 sounds plays its example word: the word the Ramp English sound chart shows, in the very clip the app plays for it (the same file, byte for byte). The voices are British text-to-speech voices from a licensed speech service, used under that service's terms, as in the app. They are computer voices, not recordings of a person. 38 clips serve the 44 sounds, because six words show two sounds: big, cat, five, chair, dog and long.

Reference vowel recordings

Nine vowels can also be heard alone, from open recordings of IPA vowels on Wikimedia Commons. They are reference samples of the IPA symbols, close to but not the same as the British English vowels, and they are not Ramp's lesson audio. Each MP3 here was converted from the Commons file (mono, 64 kbps) so that every browser can play it; each OGG is the Commons audio stream with its metadata removed. Both are shared under the licence of the original file.

Screens and drawings

Every phone and browser screen on this site is a real screenshot of Ramp Schools, cropped and never painted over. Names, class codes and school names from test accounts are cropped out; numbers in the screens are test data, not results. The road, the hills and the flag are drawn from the app's own road scene.
